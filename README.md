# Support CyShield

CyShield is a free safety app for Cyprus: shelters, hazard warnings, emergency calls, location sharing and offline maps, in English, Greek and Russian.

**It is free for everyone and always will be. No ads, no premium tiers, no paywalls.**

Running it still costs money: map servers, live alert services, and the time to keep shelter information accurate and the app up to date. If CyShield has helped you or your family feel safer, you can help keep it running for everyone in Cyprus.
