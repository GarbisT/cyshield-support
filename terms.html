3. Where the information comes from

Shelter locations come from Cyprus Civil Defence records published before the March 2026 nationwide shelter review, as republished by the safe.101.cy project. They have not been re-verified. Some shelters may have closed, become private, moved or be locked. Always check a shelter before you rely on it.

Supplementary shelter findings come from the Audit Office of the Republic of Cyprus. Weather warnings come from the Cyprus Department of Meteorology via MeteoAlarm. Earthquake data comes from the European-Mediterranean Seismological Centre (EMSC). Fire detections come from NASA FIRMS. Maps and place search come from Mapbox and OpenStreetMap contributors.

Distances and walking times are calculated by the app in a straight line. Real routes can be longer. Location accuracy depends on your device.

Feeds can be late, incomplete or unavailable, for example when you are offline or a provider has an outage. Notifications can be delayed or not delivered.
